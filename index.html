Some Answers

Get in touch

How do I contact The Baker's Basket?

DM us on Instagram @thebakersbasket, call or WhatsApp +92 321 5375875, or email abeera.wajid71@gmail.com.

Can I order a large quantity for an event?

Yes — tell us the date, the occasion and how many people, and we will confirm what we can bake for it on WhatsApp or email.

How does ordering work?

Pick your cake, send the date and design on WhatsApp or DM, and we confirm your exact price on enquiry. Pickup in Lahore or delivery via Uber car. No COD available.

What is a bento cake?

A small, personal lunchbox cake, customised with your colours and message — one of the most-ordered bakes from our posts.

What occasions do you bake for?

Birthdays, baby showers, graduations, Mother's Day, Father's Day and Eid — including our Mother's Day box of 6 cupcakes with a card and Father's Day box of 4 cupcakes with a card.

Why are there no prices on this site?

Every order is customised, so price depends on size, design and date. We confirm it before you order — we never invent a price here.

How much notice do you need?

As much as you can give us — custom cakes are made to order in a home kitchen, so message us with your date first and we will confirm availability.

What is The Baker's Basket?

A home bakery in Lahore, Pakistan, run by a mother and dentist duo — sending a slice of love, from our kitchen to you.
